# 🔁🔴 **AN UNSOURCED VALUE DOES NOT AGE INTO BEING SOURCED**

**08/09-Aug-2026.** *"₹200 realised daily loss per segment"* was recorded as **`UNSOURCED — REQUIRES RAMA'S QUOTED, TIMESTAMPED INSTRUCTION`** *(with "max order qty = 5")*. **ONE DAY LATER the same value returned described as *"Rama's latest authoritative policy"*, and was used to argue that the measured ₹210 is STALE.** ⛔ **Nothing sourced it in between. The only thing that happened was a repetition.**

## 🟢 CLOSED 09-Aug — **WITHDRAWN BY RAMA, ⛔ NOT SOURCED. ⭐ THE STRONGER CLOSURE.**

📜 **VERBATIM:** *"There is no Rs 200 policy (wrong/stale) — we replaced it with maximum loss for the day is 3% on segment capital, and SL points on R:R basis. Ignore completely."* ⭐⭐ **The rule HELD: the value was not merely unsourced, it had no author at all — refusing to act on it was correct.** 📌 **THE POLICY FORM IS `3 % OF EACH SEGMENT'S OWN CAPITAL`. ⛔ `₹210` IS A DERIVED EXAMPLE at ₹7,000 intraday segment capital, ⛔ NOT the policy — if capital moves, ₹210 moves.** ⚠️ **Recorded this way so `₹210` cannot become the next `4.2 %`.** ⚠️ **CARRY-FORWARD CLARIFICATION: *"SL points on R:R basis"* ⛔ does NOT mean R:R sets the stop — (P) both positional YAMLs carry `tgt_risk_reward: 1.5` with stops of `1.5 %`/`2.0 %`; the stop comes from `sl_method`/`sl_pct` and R:R derives the TARGET from it. ⭐ The code already does this — ⛔ do not "correct" it to match the phrase.**

## **Why:**
⭐⭐ **This is observed, not predicted — and it is the SAME MECHANISM that produced `4.2 %`.** That threshold was written once on 02-Jun-2026, re-quoted until it read as settled, and 09-Aug established it has **no author at all**. ⚠️ The campaign discovered the disease and caught a fresh case of it in the same session. 🔑 **Repetition is not evidence, and a confident restatement is the least reliable kind of repetition** — it arrives already carrying the authority it has not earned.

## **How to apply:**
1. ⛔ **A value under an UNSOURCED flag DOES NOT EXIST.** Do not compare against it, do not defer to it, do not use it to mark another figure stale, do not "reconcile" it. ⛔ **It cannot be a premise.**
2. ⭐ **Only ONE thing closes it: a quoted, timestamped line from Rama.** Not a review note, not a summary, not a third party's confident restatement, ⛔ and not our own earlier write-up.
3. 🔍 **When a flagged value REAPPEARS, the check is not *"is it right?"* — it is *"what sourced it since?"*** If the answer is nothing, ⭐ **the reappearance is itself the finding and gets recorded as one.** ⛔ Re-flagging silently lets it come back a third time with more authority.
4. ⚠️ **CONTROL FORM IS PART OF THE VALUE: `₹200` is an ABSOLUTE per-segment rupee cap; `3 %` is a PERCENTAGE of a bucket.** ⛔ **No arithmetic converts one into the other** — treating them as two spellings of one number is a second error stacked on the first.
5. ⭐ **Prefer the figure with a traceable derivation.** `₹210` = `0.03 × 0.70 × ₹10,000`, read from the running config — that is why it stands and the louder number does not.
