## What it does
NEW `orders/structure_exit_manager.py` `StructureExitManager` — push-driven by `CandleStore.register_on_candle_close` (1m closes, multi-subscriber, paper-parity). For each OPEN **MIS LIMIT_TRIPLE** trade on its symbol:
- **ACTION B (break, evaluated FIRST):** a 1m CLOSE beyond the nearest HIGH band (`±break_buffer_pct`) + a STRONG close → EXIT. Polarity flip vs the entry confirm: LONG support-break = strong LOWER close (`retest_confirm._strong_close(is_long=False)`), SHORT resistance-break = strong UPPER. Exit in the MANDATORY order: (1) `trades.status='EXITING'`, (2) cancel resting SL+TGT, (3) reverse-aware marketable-LIMIT flatten (MARKET fallback; broker-flat ⇒ skip = no double-exit). EXITING-first inherits the reconciler CHECK1/G5b exclusion (no spurious SL re-arm).
- **ACTION A (trail, else):** new SL = `support.band_low×(1−sl_buffer_pct/100)` LONG / `resistance.band_high×(1+…)` SHORT, tick-snapped; LIMIT moved with the trigger (`calc_sl_limit_price`); **two real PRE-modify guards** ONLY-TIGHTEN + WRONG-SIDE-vs-LTP (root-cause, mirrors `smart_tgt_manager.py:861` — NOT broker-rejection reliance); **debounce ≤1 move per (trade, candle.ts)**.

## Key design decisions (mine, beyond the brief)
- **Zone selection keys on the candle EXTREME (high LONG / low SHORT), not the close.** Critical fix: on the breaking candle `close<band_low`, so a close-keyed selection would EXCLUDE the very zone being broken → ACTION B could never fire. The extreme (candle traded above the support, then closed below) keeps trail+break coherent on one selected zone.
- **BreakevenManager is fully dormant** (not instantiated in main.py, not passed to OrderPlacer = None, no strategy sets `trailing_sl_enabled`; `broker_order_id` is only ever an ALIAS of `order_id` — its `SELECT broker_order_id FROM orders` only "works" because it's mock-tested). So structure-exit is the SOLE SL-leg writer = **single SL owner** (do NOT co-enable `trailing_sl_enabled`). I use `order_id` (the broker-assigned PK) for modify+cancel.
- Flatten is **parity-safe**: exit_side from the LOCAL trade direction (live qty is signed, paper qty is abs — can't trust the sign), qty prefers broker truth, broker-flat ⇒ skip.

## Scope / invariants
MIS LIMIT_TRIPLE only — CO (SL in bracket) + CNC (SL in OCO GTT) EXCLUDED; delivery off. HIGH-confidence zones only; no-zone ⇒ leave SL.
