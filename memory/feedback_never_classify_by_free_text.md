**⭐ NEVER CLASSIFY, COUNT, FILTER OR GROUP BY FREE TEXT WHEN A STRUCTURED STATUS EXISTS.**
Rendering the free text to a human is correct and expected. **BRANCHING on its contents is the
defect.** Recorded 18-Jul-2026 after it shipped to production and stayed there.

**THE CASE.** `reports/daily_report.py:464` did
`"CAPITAL" in (s.get("rejection_reason") or "").upper()`. The CONCENTRATION rejection reason
embeds `capital_qty=`:

    "qty=0: CONCENTRATION exhausted for GRAVITA (risk_qty=6 capital_qty=17 conc_qty=0)"

⇒ the row read **"Rejected (Capital)"** for rejections that were **100% CONCENTRATION**, while
concentration — the constraint that binds on **100% of trades** — was named nowhere the operator
could see. `:549` then GROUPED by that same string; because it embeds the symbol and three arm
values, **7,655 rejections became 190 near-unique lines instead of 9**, each too small to notice.

**⚠️ THE PREMISE NEEDED CORRECTING, AND SAYING SO MATTERED.** The brief expected "3,098 → 0".
Measured: **the count was MISLABELLED, not miscounted** — all 3,098 sizing rejections are
CONCENTRATION and no non-sizing reason contains "capital", so the substring picked the right
ROWS for the wrong REASON. The fix makes it right **by construction** instead of by coincidence.
*Report the number you measure, not the number you were told to expect*
([[feedback-verify-the-finding-premise]]).

**THE SWEEP MATTERS MORE THAN THE PATCH.** Fixing the two reported lines would have left two
more of the same class live: `:465` counted only `"REJECTED" in status`, so **2,688** signals
carrying `DROPPED_*`/`SKIPPED_*`/`QUEUE_FULL`/`PLACEMENT_FAILED`/`TIMEOUT` were reported as
*silently dead*; `:459` conflated the dedup `DUPLICATE` with `REJECTED_DUPLICATE_SYMBOL`.

**How to apply:**
1. **Classify off the enum.** `signals.status` is generated by the system itself
   (`signal_processor` raises `_PipelineReject(f"SIZING_{constraint}")` → `REJECTED_SIZING_*`),
   so prefix/family matching on it is *contractual*. Substring matching on a human sentence is
   incidental and breaks the moment the sentence changes.
2. **One classifier, not many** (Rule #4). `reports/signal_status.py` is now the single place;
   `daily_trade_review._signal_bucket` already had the right pattern and was **promoted** into
   it rather than duplicated. A test asserts the delegation.
3. **Guard the reintroduction** with a source scan — but scope it to CODE, not comments:
   documenting the retired predicate must stay legal.
4. **Beware status-side fragmentation too.** `REJECTED_SCORE_<nn>` is ~40 distinct statuses;
   grouping by raw status fragments just as badly. Collapse known families.
5. **Ask whether any consumer is PROGRAMMATIC before changing counts.** Here none was (the xlsx
   is checked for existence/size and served for human download; "Auto Tuning Signals" emits
   advisory strings only) ⇒ presentation-only. If one had been, it would have been a behaviour
   change and needed re-scoping.
6. **Don't change what you cannot show is wrong.** Three `event_type` sites use the same pattern
   (`"CRITICAL" in event_type` counts routine `KILL_AUTO_CLEARED`) but that is *ambiguous
   intent*, not demonstrable misclassification ⇒ reported, not changed, on the eve of a live
   boot. Sibling of [[feedback-live-vs-latent-findings]].
