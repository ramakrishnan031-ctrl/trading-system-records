### Facts bearing on "config change or build" (stated, not recommended)

1. **The detector cannot take a week.** Adding `week` to `sr_detector.timeframes` is rejected at
   config load, and the boot returns 5 (E2).
2. **The V3 chain would accept one syntactically.**
   - `v3_chain.structure_intervals` has **no** interval validator (`core/config_loader.py:1477-1478`).
   - The validators at `:1484-1513` check only mode, combine, atr30_period, rr_floor, exec_budget
     and thresholds.
   - A `week` entry would therefore pass config load and reach `kite.historical_data(interval="week")`
     verbatim through `main.py:432-437`.
   - Whether Kite serves that interval **cannot be measured** in this pass (see the last section).
   - That chain is LOG-ONLY (`v3_chain/runner.py:1-14`).
3. **The vendor library does not check intervals.** `kiteconnect` **5.1.0** (the VM venv) substitutes
   `interval` into `/instruments/historical/{instrument_token}/{interval}` (`connect.py:151`, `:644`).
   Its docstring lists only *"minute, day, 5 minute etc."* (`connect.py:635`).
4. **The daily series is 180 days.** The daily series fetched for S&R spans 180 calendar days
   (`:511`, `:614`). The contract's 400-day lookback [A] appears nowhere in the S&R path; the only
   400 is `regime.daily_lookback_days` (`:551`), which is the NIFTY index and disabled.
5. **No weekly role exists.** `TF_ROLE` at `sr_detector/models.py:180-186` has day, 60minute,
   30minute, 15minute and 5minute only.
